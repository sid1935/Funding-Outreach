Funding Outreach

Finds seed and pre-seed announcements, scores them against the accelerator thesis, and drafts personalised outreach. Drafts only: nothing is ever sent automatically.

How it works

  1. Scans ~50 news feeds and searches for funding announcements from the last 24 hours.
  2. Gemini extracts the startup and keeps only seed and pre-seed rounds.
  3. Already-contacted startups are skipped (unique constraints in the database).
  4. Gemini scores fit against the thesis below, read fresh from Supabase on every run.
  5. Fits get a researched, personalised draft in Rohit's Gmail. Non-fits are never shown.
